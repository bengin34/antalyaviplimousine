Flight Tracking & Delays

We monitor incoming flights directly.

If a flight is delayed, the pickup time is automatically adjusted according to the actual arrival time.

There is no additional waiting charge for flight delays.

Even in the case of a significant delay, no extra delay fee is charged as long as the scheduled flight eventually operates.

If the airline cancels the flight completely, the corresponding airport transfer can also be cancelled without a cancellation charge.

Booking & Confirmation

For destinations with an established fixed price, reservations are confirmed immediately.

Once a reservation is submitted:

1. The booking is registered in our operational system.
2. All passenger, flight, hotel and transfer information is recorded.
3. The reservation is confirmed.
4. A confirmation message is generated.

For B2B partners, we can send the confirmation directly to you instead of the passenger.

You may then continue all normal communication with your own customer and provide your own booking confirmation or voucher.

⸻

Customer Communication

You remain the main commercial contact for your customer.

Antalya VIP Tourism will only contact the passenger when operationally necessary, for example:

* Airport pickup coordination
* Driver arrival
* Difficulty locating the passenger
* Flight changes
* Pickup changes
* Urgent transfer-day communication

We do not take over the commercial relationship with your customer.

⸻

Vehicle & Driver Information

The booked vehicle category is guaranteed.

The final vehicle assignment is normally completed several hours before the transfer.

Once the vehicle has been allocated, we can send:

* Vehicle type
* Vehicle photographs
* Relevant pickup information

Exact vehicle and driver allocation may change during daily operations.

Driver coordination is handled internally by Antalya VIP Tourism. B2B partners and passengers do not communicate directly with drivers outside the operational process unless required.

If an assigned driver becomes unavailable, we arrange another driver. Our operational capacity allows us to replace vehicles or drivers when necessary rather than cancelling the transfer.

Payment

Our listed B2B prices apply when the passenger pays the transfer amount directly to the driver.

Accepted currencies:

* EUR
* USD
* TRY

EUR and USD payments are accepted directly.

USD amounts are converted using the current applicable exchange rate.

TRY payments are converted using our operational cash exchange rate, which may be higher than the standard bank or online exchange rate.

For this reason, we recommend EUR payment whenever possible.

If payment needs to be collected in advance by Antalya VIP Tourism through bank transfer, card payment or invoicing, different prices may apply because of additional tax and processing costs.

⸻

Commission & Agency Markup

We do not pay a separate commission to B2B partners.

Instead, we provide an agreed B2B transfer price.

You are free to add your own:

* Agency markup
* Booking fee
* Service fee
* Package margin

to the price you charge your customer.

Your selling price to your customer is your own commercial decision.

Cancellation Policy

More than 48 hours before pickup

Free cancellation.

Less than 48 hours before pickup

50% of the confirmed transfer price is payable.

The cancellation fee must be transferred to the bank account provided by Antalya VIP Tourism.

No-show

If the passenger does not appear for the confirmed transfer and the transfer cannot be completed:

100% of the transfer price is payable.

The no-show amount is requested by bank transfer on the same day.

Flight cancellation

If the passenger’s airline cancels the flight completely:

No cancellation payment is required.

Flight delays do not result in additional charges.

Failure to settle valid cancellation or no-show charges may result in suspension or termination of the B2B cooperation.
