Travelling to your hotel with children

Child seats are free on request, but only if we know about them before you land. What to tell us, and what actually fits in the vehicle.

Updated 2026-09-16·4 min read

A transfer with small children is a logistics problem, not a price problem. Seats, a stroller, a travel cot and four suitcases have to fit in the same vehicle at the same time - and the decisions that make that work are taken at booking, not at the terminal.

Child seats

We provide child seats free of charge on request. Tell us the number of children and their ages when you book; that is what determines whether you need an infant carrier, a toddler seat or a booster. Seats are prepared with the vehicle, so there is nothing to carry through the airport and nothing to arrange at 01:00 in the arrivals hall.

What fits in the vehicle

  • Mercedes Vito: up to six passengers with normal holiday luggage.
  • Mercedes Sprinter: larger groups, up to 12 seats, and the right choice when a stroller and travel cot travel with you.
  • Strollers and car seats do not count against the passenger number, but they do take luggage space - tell us and we assign the vehicle accordingly.

The fixed price is for the vehicle, not per seat, so adding a child never changes the fare. What changes is which vehicle we send.

Why private matters more with children

On a shared shuttle a family waits for the vehicle to fill, then rides along the coast while other parties are dropped off. With a toddler after a night flight, that is the difference between forty minutes and three hours. A private vehicle leaves when you are ready and drives straight to your hotel reception.

Practical details worth knowing

Drinking water is available in the vehicle. If someone needs a short stop on a long run to Side or Alanya, just ask the driver - there is no schedule to keep. And because payment is in cash at the start of the journey, nobody has to find a card or a signal while holding a sleeping child.

Frequently asked questions

Are child seats free?

Yes. Child seats are provided at no extra cost on request. Please give the number of children and their ages when booking.

Can I bring a stroller?

Yes. Tell us at booking so we allow luggage space for it - a stroller plus a full set of suitcases may mean a Sprinter rather than a Vito.

Do children count towards the passenger limit?

Yes, for seat capacity. The price does not change: it is fixed per vehicle, not per person.

Can we stop on a long transfer?

Yes. On a private transfer the driver can make a short stop on request; there are no other passengers waiting.
