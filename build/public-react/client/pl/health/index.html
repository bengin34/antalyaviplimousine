One point of contact, two distinct roles

Coordination is not medical care.

Keeping this boundary visible is essential. We organise the journey; authorised medical professionals assess and treat you.

Antalya VIP Tourism coordinates

  • Airport, hotel and provider transfers
  • Accommodation and companion logistics
  • Appointment and daily itinerary flow
  • Language and communication assistance where arranged
  • Non-clinical changes to the travel plan

The healthcare provider manages

  • Diagnosis and medical suitability
  • Choice and scope of treatment
  • Risks, alternatives and recovery guidance
  • Informed consent, procedures and medication
  • Clinical follow-up and complication management

Antalya VIP Tourism is not a healthcare provider and does not diagnose, recommend a treatment method or promise a medical or aesthetic outcome.

Your journey

Six clear steps from first call to return.

Each handover is defined so you always know who you are speaking to and what happens next.

  1. 01

    Initial conversation

    We learn your travel expectations, preferred language and approximate dates without requesting detailed health data.

  2. 02

    Provider introduction

    The healthcare provider responsible for the assessment is identified before records are shared.

  3. 03

    Consent and records

    Only necessary information is transferred to the named provider after the relevant privacy and consent information is clear.

  4. 04

    Physician pre-assessment

    The clinical team gives an initial view. This is not a final diagnosis, treatment approval or result guarantee.

  5. 05

    Written plan

    Clinical and travel costs, inclusions, exclusions, timing and possible changes are presented separately.

  6. 06

    Arrival and follow-up

    The final plan follows in-person assessment. Before return, the provider supplies aftercare and contact instructions.

Provider selection

What we look for before making an introduction.

A polished lobby is not evidence of clinical quality. Provider identity, authority, physician responsibility and continuity of care matter more.

  • Verifiable legal identity and physical address
  • Relevant international health-tourism authorisation
  • Named physician and clearly stated specialty
  • Written consent, risk and alternatives process
  • Transparent clinical and non-clinical pricing
  • Documented discharge and follow-up route
